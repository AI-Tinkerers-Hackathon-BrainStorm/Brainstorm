# 2–3 minute demo

## Before the room opens

Place a red bottle on the left side of a well-lit desk and a printed sign near the camera. Open the deployed HTTPS URL on a phone, allow camera and microphone, and confirm the header says ONLINE. Keep the Agent View closed until the reveal.

## 0:00–0:40 — explain the difference

Say: “SightLoop is not a photo describer. It holds a goal, observes again, acts, and verifies.” Start the camera. Point out that the preview is smooth while the AI indicator is around one frame per second.

## 0:40–1:25 — Find

Say: “Help me find my red bottle.” Pan slowly. SightLoop should guide left/right, vibrate when supported, enter VERIFYING after the first match, and announce the result only after the second supporting observation. Open Agent View to show goal, observation, decision, action, latency, dropped stale frames, and the timeline.

## 1:25–1:55 — Read

Say: “Read the sign in front of me.” Hold the phone steady. SightLoop captures one high-resolution frame, routes it to Qwen OCR, displays the transcript, and reads useful text aloud.

## 1:55–2:25 — Memory

Say: “Remember where I put my keys,” place the keys beside a laptop, then turn the camera away. Ask: “Where are my keys?” Expected wording: “I last saw what appeared to be your keys beside the laptop. I can’t confirm they’re still there.” Emphasize that history is not presented as current fact.

## 2:25–2:50 — resilience

Turn on Low bandwidth mode. Automatic frames stop while the preview stays live. Press Scan now to show the single-frame fallback. Close with the experimental-assistance notice.
